Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoon melted butter

Instrustions:

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Whisk together the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients and mix until smooth.
  4. Cook on a hot pan until bubbles form and and flip.
  5. Serve hot with syrup or favourite toppings.

Finished yummy pancakes. enjoy!
